# Authors and contributors

Guppty was created and is maintained by Adrian Cardona.

Contributors are credited through the Git history and release notes. A contribution belongs to the
person who made it; generated or assisted work should still be reviewed and submitted by a human
who can stand behind it. Please use the same name you want people to see in `git shortlog`.

By contributing, you agree to license your contribution under Guppty's MIT License. Add yourself to
this file when you make a meaningful code, language-design, documentation, testing, or research
contribution.
